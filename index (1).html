Step 3 — Your trip i
Why these matter: bus and train tickets are per person, but a car is per vehicle — one person on a bus often beats driving, four people rarely does. And if you're leaving a car at the airport, the number of days you're away is usually the biggest cost of the whole trip.

A separate flight just to reach the starting city. Example — you're in Chicago, you fly to Seattle, then travel on to Vancouver to catch your cheaper flight. The Chicago → Seattle flight is that cost. Enter the total for everyone travelling.
